Avengers: Endgame

Introduction

Released in 2019, Avengers: Endgame is a superhero film from Marvel Studios. It stands as a central entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and carries forward the narrative following Avengers: Infinity War.

Plot

After Thanos eliminates half of all life across the universe, the remaining Avengers grapple with their loss. Upon finding a potential method to undo the event, they gather for one last mission to bring back those who vanished and face Thanos again.

Cast

Main Characters

  • Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark/Iron Man
  • Chris Evans as Steve Rogers / Captain America
  • Chris Hemsworth as Thor
  • Mark Ruffalo as Bruce Banner / Hulk
  • Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow
  • Jeremy Renner as Clint Barton / Hawkeye

My Review

Avengers: Endgame ranks among my favorite films because it gathers numerous characters and narratives from earlier Marvel releases. I appreciated the action sequences, emotional beats, and particularly the final clash. The picture also offers meaningful resolutions for several key figures. Overall, I found the experience thrilling, memorable, and pleasant to view.
